## 🔹 Task 4 — Data Storytelling & Statistical Validation

**Focus:** Converting analytical findings into a business narrative and validating a key relationship statistically.

### Key Analysis

The analysis examined the relationship between:

**Quantity Sold ↔ Total Sales**

### Statistical Result

**Pearson correlation:** approximately **0.647**

The statistical test produced an extremely small p-value, providing strong evidence that the observed relationship is statistically significant.

### Business Interpretation

The analysis indicates a meaningful positive relationship between quantity sold and total sales, supporting the business relevance of sales-volume analysis.

### Deliverables

- Final presentation
- Hypothesis testing summary
- Python statistical analysis
- Business insights and conclusions
